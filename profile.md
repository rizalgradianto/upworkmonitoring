# Profile Preferences

This file only holds things the live Upwork profile can't express — what to avoid, tone, and
explicit overrides. Skills, rate, overview, and work history always come fresh from the
Upwork connector's `get_profile` — don't duplicate that data here, and don't let this file
go stale relative to it.

## Niche
Marketing websites & landing pages — Webflow, Framer, WordPress, Shopify. Figma-first design
with developer handoff, for agencies, startups, and SaaS/e-commerce brands. Structure-first,
conversion-focused.

## What to avoid
- Pure development/coding jobs with no design component (bug fixes, backend-only work, plugin
  troubleshooting, API integrations)
- Full custom app or SaaS product engineering — this is a websites/landing pages profile, not
  a software dev one
- Content writing, copywriting-only, SEO-only, or video editing gigs
- Adult content, gambling, or other niches outside comfort zone
- Micro-budget jobs (e.g. fixed price under ~$50, "$2/page" style gigs) unless the fit is
  unusually strong
- Design-from-scratch asks with a budget/timeline that doesn't match the scope

## Tone for proposal drafts
- Direct and confident — no generic filler ("I am excited to apply", "I have carefully read
  your job post")
- Structure first, visuals second — matches how I actually work
- Honest about fit — if it's a partial match, say so instead of overselling
- Concise: a few tight sentences, not a wall of text
